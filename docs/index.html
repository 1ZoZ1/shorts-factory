03 / AUTOMATION WITH REVIEW

Build the workflow.
Keep your judgment.

The implemented QA gate checks the file, duration and captions. Your review covers the story, factual accuracy, media rights and the finished experience.

Generation ends at QA. YouTube and TikTok publishing use separate commands. An interrupted job can resume, while a changed brief needs a fresh job or a deliberate rebuild.
